What is Causal Inference?

Beyond simple correlation.

Correlation

"When A happens, B usually happens too."

Example: Ice cream sales and shark attacks both go up in summer.

Causality

"A causes B to happen."

Example: Heat causes ice cream sales AND heat causes people to swim (sharks).

Causal Inference is the science of proving "Why" things happen, not just "What" happened.

Why it Matters for Business

  • Stop Wasting Budget: Don't optimize for metrics that don't drive revenue.
  • Predict Outcomes: If we change price by $1, what is the true effect on churn?
  • Risk Mitigation: Identify hidden "confounders" (external factors) that could crash your strategy.
  • Real-Time Strategy: Reactive data analysis is too slow; autonomous discovery catches trends as they form.
